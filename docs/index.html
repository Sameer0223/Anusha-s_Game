How to play
  • Two players join the same room ID.
  • Players take turns adding an orb to any empty cell or their own cell.
  • Cells have a limit: corners 2, edges 3, center 4.
  • When a cell reaches its limit, it explodes and sends orbs to neighbors.
  • Explosions can chain across the board.
  • Capture all opponent orbs to win!
